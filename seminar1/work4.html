Instructions

This application calculates the specific location (Entrance and Floor) of an apartment based on its door number.

  • M: Total number of entrances in the building.
  • O: Total number of floors per entrance.
  • K: Number of apartment doors on each floor.
  • N: The door number you are looking for.
